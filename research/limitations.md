# Limits and interpretation

1. **Primary paper access:** S01 and S02 were not independently read. The scientific literature-foundation milestone remains incomplete. No AlphaQubit accuracy, architecture or latency comparison is claimed.
2. **Small code and short memory:** one distance (3), one memory basis (Z), three measurement rounds, one logical observable. No distance scaling, threshold, hardware transfer or per-round extrapolation is supported.
3. **Noise family:** Stim circuit-level Pauli noise with controlled pre-measurement flips. Not leakage, arbitrary non-Markovian drift, coherent noise, a calibrated device, or general quantum-state simulation. The measurement parameter also applies where the generated circuit measures final data, as specified in the retained circuit.
4. **Input and target:** the model predicts a logical-observable flip from detector bits. It produces neither a unique physical recovery chain nor ground-truth fault locations. The replay's simulator label is privileged information revealed for evaluation.
5. **Data coverage:** three model initializations share one training dataset and the same final test set per condition. These are not three independent physical experiments. Test Wilson intervals exclude training uncertainty, model selection uncertainty and noise-model error. No causal claim about hardware is made.
6. **Class imbalance and probabilities:** most shots have no logical flip. Sparse reliability bins are unstable. Brier score blends calibration and discrimination; the observed increase is not a standalone proof of a unique calibration mechanism. No calibration correction was fitted after seeing final tests.
7. **Baselines:** ordinary MWPM on decomposed DEMs need not capture every correlation. Matched prior is given the test noise model. Neural and matching hyperparameters have not been jointly optimized under equal extensive search budgets.
8. **Statistics:** descriptive finite-sample comparisons, no hypothesis-test significance claim and no multiple-comparison correction. Zero failures would not establish zero probability. Error is per complete experiment, never renamed per round.
9. **Timing:** local CPU batched `predict_proba` timing excludes simulation, I/O, preprocessing outside the call and browser replay; amortized batch throughput is not single-shot hardware latency. No real-time deployment claim.
10. **Reproducibility:** scripts, pinned versions, configuration, random streams, aggregates and hashes are retained. Bitwise identity may depend on numerical library and platform. Large datasets are regenerated, not included. Manuscript values refer to the retained run ID.
11. **Authorship:** independent computational work produced in this repository with AI engineering assistance. No institutional affiliation, external collaborators, peer review or hardware access is asserted.
12. **Fresh-shot confirmation:** the 100,000-shot-per-condition extension fixes the original seed101 weights, decision threshold and noise family. New seeds reduce conditional test-sampling uncertainty, but do not replicate training, estimate architecture uncertainty or validate a device. Its protocol is committed before fresh labels but was designed after the original study; original and confirmation counts are not pooled.
13. **Harder noise versus mismatch:** increasing measurement noise changes the underlying inference problem as well as moving away from the training distribution. The observed sweep does not isolate the excess cost specifically attributable to mismatch. A separate exploratory matched-training control now provides a within-noise procedural comparison; it does not retroactively change what the original sweep identifies. Matched-prior matching is a different model family with privileged noise information, not that neural counterfactual. The frozen-model confirmation reduces sampling uncertainty but does not add this missing control.
14. **Post-hoc confidence diagnostics:** event probability is not classification confidence. Risk/coverage thresholds and confidence bins were fixed for a descriptive analysis after parent results were known; the same original/confirmation labels are reused. Subsets overlap, pointwise intervals are not simultaneous bands, and no selective threshold is recommended for real-time use. These diagnostics do not add independent evidence or identify mismatch causality.

15. **Exploratory training control:** all twelve models are new fits; fixed training reuses the new 0.005 group, not the original seed101 checkpoint. Evaluation noise is known to the matched-training procedure. One train/validation dataset per noise and three initialization/shuffling seeds do not replicate whole training datasets. Different sampled training data, finite optimization and model capacity limit any mismatch-specific causal interpretation. At 0.005 the procedures are identical by construction; at 0.001 matched training does not improve observed logical error. Original, confirmation and control tests are not pooled.
