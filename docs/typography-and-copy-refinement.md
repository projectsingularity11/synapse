# Typography and copy refinement

El Messiri replaces Syne as the display face. The unmodified variable font comes from the official Google Fonts repository, with its OFL and SHA256 manifest retained under public/fonts. Interface and paper body text retain Space Grotesk for readability.

Homepage headings now name the research topic and sections directly. Four explanation headings name encoding, parity measurements, detector history, and decoder architecture. Promotional section introductions are replaced by specific descriptions of the available materials. Scientific descriptions, numerical evidence, and caveats remain intact.

White gradients and white primary controls establish the accent hierarchy over black. Red-orange remains in the experimental scene and small interaction signals. Cursor fluid opacity, force, radius, brightness, fallback opacity, spread, and lifetime are reduced. Ambient motion still uses transforms and respects reduced motion. Previous frame-cadence records predate this refinement and are not new performance measurements.
