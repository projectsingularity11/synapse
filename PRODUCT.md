# Synapse

Synapse is independent research by Assirem Benseddik: an interactive explanation of a small simulated quantum-memory decoding study for curious beginners. Visitors first learn what bits, qubits, encoded memories and error clues mean, manipulate clearly labeled teaching examples, then inspect saved detector histories, measured results, uncertainty and exact methods. Preserve the frozen experiment artifacts, counts and limitations. The study uses classical computation; it is not quantum hardware or a replication of published large decoders.

The owner requested an immersive ink-dark site with electric blue and hot pink, self-hosted Syne display type and Manrope body type, substantial motion, liquid atmosphere and glass surfaces.
